\## Business Problem



Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded daily sales and stock information for two weeks. She needs to understand how her business is performing, whether sales are growing, which days perform strongly or weakly, and which products need to be restocked. This analysis uses core Python to turn the sales and inventory records into practical business insights. The findings can help Wanjiku make better decisions about stock management, sales planning, and where to focus her efforts.



\## How to Run



Open the notebook directly in Google Colab: \[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/kimirifelix-droid/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall\_analysis.ipynb)



Download or upload `stall\_analysis.ipynb` to Colab and run all cells from top to bottom.



\## Top Three Findings



1\. Total sales over the 14 days were \*\*KES 46,450\*\*, with average daily sales of \*\*KES 3,317.86\*\*.



2\. Week 2 sales increased to \*\*KES 24,400\*\*, representing \*\*10.66% growth\*\* compared with Week 1 sales of KES 22,050.



3\. Weekend average sales were \*\*KES 4,900\*\*, which was \*\*82.50% higher\*\* than the weekday average of KES 2,685.00. Tomatoes and Sukuma also require restocking because their quantities are at or below their reorder levels.
